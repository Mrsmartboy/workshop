# Codegnan Labs
## Website Review & Developer Revision Brief

**For:** Codegnan Colleges / Campus Programs
**Website reviewed:** codegnan-workshop.vercel.app
**Prepared for developer implementation** | September 2026

---

## Primary Objective

Reposition the website from a generic training/events website into a clear Codegnan Labs campus-engagement property built around Workshops, Bootcamps and Hackathons for colleges and students.

**Target outcome:** a website that immediately answers "What is Codegnan Labs?", builds trust with college decision-makers, excites students, and drives campus partnership enquiries.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The current website has a strong visual base and a useful three-program structure, but the positioning is not yet focused enough for the Codegnan Colleges initiative. It currently feels like a mix of student training, professional events and a Codegnan center website. The revision should make one proposition dominant: **Codegnan Labs brings hands-on technology experiences directly to college campuses.**

### Recommended Brand Architecture

Codegnan = parent brand | Codegnan Labs = campus innovation & technology initiative | Workshops / Bootcamps / Hackathons = core experiences

## 3. Required Positioning & Hero Rewrite

The first screen must explain the business within seconds. A visitor should know who this is for, what Codegnan Labs does, and what action to take without scrolling.

### Recommended Positioning

> **CODEGNAN LABS**
> Campus Innovation & Technology Experiences
>
> We bring industry learning to your campus.
> Workshops • Hackathons • Bootcamps
>
> Hands-on technology experiences that help college students learn, build, compete and discover what they are capable of.
>
> **Primary CTA:** BRING CODEGNAN TO YOUR CAMPUS
> **Secondary CTA:** EXPLORE PROGRAMS

### Messaging Principles

- Use simple, energetic language. Avoid corporate filler and long paragraphs.
- Lead with student experience and tangible outcomes, not course catalog language.
- Speak to both students and college management without making the homepage feel split.
- Use "we come to your campus" as a repeated differentiator.
- Use real Codegnan proof wherever a claim is made.
- Keep the emotional arc: Explore → Learn → Build → Compete → Showcase.

### What to Remove or De-emphasize

- Business-professional event positioning on the main Colleges homepage.
- Center-specific event emphasis such as "visit our Vijayawada training center" as a primary proposition.
- Generic statements such as "world-class teachers" unless supported by specific proof.
- Any placeholder / lorem ipsum content.
- Placement-first language in the hero or first sections.

## 5. College Decision-Maker Experience

The website must answer the practical questions a Principal, Dean, HOD, TPO or Training Head will have before they submit an enquiry.

### Recommended "Bring Codegnan Labs to Your College" Matrix

| Experience | Typical Duration | Primary Purpose | Student Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tech Workshop | 2-6 hours | Explore a technology | Guided build / exposure |
| Bootcamp | 1-5 days | Develop a practical skill | Hands-on project / skill evidence |
| Hackathon | 1-2 days | Build and compete | Team solution / recognition |
| College Tech Fest | Custom | Large-scale engagement | Multi-track campus experience |

### Lead Form Fields

Keep the form short enough to complete quickly, but useful enough for the Codegnan team to qualify the enquiry.

- College Name
- City
- Contact Person
- Designation
- Phone
- Email
- Expected Student Count
- Interested In: Workshop / Bootcamp / Hackathon / Tech Fest

### Conversion Rule

Every major section should naturally point toward one of two actions: "Bring Codegnan to Your Campus" or "Explore Programs". Avoid multiple competing CTAs.

## 6. Content & Credibility Requirements

### Replace Generic "Why Us" Content With

**Industry Practitioners**
Students learn from trainers who teach current tools and industry-relevant practices.

**Build, Don't Just Listen**
Every experience should include hands-on challenges and a tangible output.

**Designed for College Students**
Experiences can be adapted for first-year exploration, skill development or placement-oriented cohorts.

**Large-Scale Execution**
Programs should communicate the ability to run across departments, years and larger student groups.

### Proof Elements to Add

- Verified Codegnan metrics only - students trained, placements, college partnerships, workshops, hackathons or other substantiated numbers.
- Real college and partner logos with permission.
- Real photos from past campus activities rather than generic stock imagery.
- Examples of student projects, GitHub work, hackathon solutions and certificates.
- Short testimonials from students and college representatives, ideally naming role + institution.
- Case-study cards showing college, program, student count, duration and outcome.

### Placement Claim Guidance

If statements such as "850+ hiring partners", interview recommendations or placement benefits are retained, they should be verified, contextualized and placed later in the page. The Codegnan Labs promise should remain centered on hands-on experience and demonstrated skills.

## 8. Definition of Done

The revision is ready for review when the homepage satisfies the following acceptance criteria:

- [ ] A new visitor understands Codegnan Labs and the campus proposition within the first screen.
- [ ] The homepage consistently serves the college-program context; no unrelated professional-events positioning remains in the primary flow.
- [ ] The primary CTA "Bring Codegnan to Your Campus" is visible without scrolling and repeats at logical conversion points.
- [ ] College decision-makers can understand formats, typical durations and expected outcomes without contacting Codegnan first.
- [ ] All placeholder and generic content has been replaced.
- [ ] All public numerical claims and partner claims are internally verified.
- [ ] Real Codegnan evidence is visible: metrics, photos, logos, outcomes, projects or testimonials.
- [ ] The lead form works on mobile and desktop and captures required information.
- [ ] Page is responsive, fast and visually consistent across common desktop and mobile widths.
- [ ] CTA and form analytics are instrumented for launch measurement.

### Final Direction to Development

Do not redesign everything. Preserve the strongest parts of the current visual system, but rebuild the information hierarchy around Codegnan Labs, campus delivery, real student experiences and college conversion. The objective is **clarity + credibility + action**.

> **Recommended launch message:** "Codegnan Labs - Workshops, Bootcamps and Hackathons, brought to your campus."
